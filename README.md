# 🏢 Apex Real Estate Group — Trifold Business Brochure Design

A publication-quality, 6-panel trifold business brochure created as part of a Graphic Design & Marketing Internship project. This project showcases structured layout design, visual hierarchy, typography, and professional brand identity.

---

## 📐 Project Architecture & Specifications

* **Format:** Standard 11" × 8.5" Landscape (US Letter)
* **Fold Type:** Standard 3-Panel Trifold (6 Panels Total across 2 Spreads)
* **Panel Width:** 3.66 inches per panel
* **Resolution / Print Readiness:** High-DPI / Vector PDF output with safety margins and grid alignment
* **Design Philosophy:** Modern corporate elegance combining deep navy tones with warm gold accents and clear visual hierarchy.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Visual System

* **Primary Dark Navy (`#0F172A`):** Conveys trust, authority, and corporate stability.
* **Warm Gold Accent (`#D97706`):** Adds luxury framing, highlight badges, and call-to-action emphasis.
* **Soft Gold Background (`#FEF3C7`):** Tints feature cards and testimonial callouts.
* **Slate Gray Text (`#334155`):** High-contrast body typography for optimal readability.

---

## 📑 Brochure Layout Breakdown

### **Page 1: Outside Spread**
1. **Inside Flap (Left Panel):** *"Why Choose Apex"* — Highlights key industry statistics ($2.5B+ in sales, 15+ years experience, 99% retention rate) and core value pillars.
2. **Back Cover (Middle Panel):** *"Connect With Us"* — Brand emblem, corporate address, contact details, social media handles, legal copyright, and QR code placeholder.
3. **Front Cover (Right Panel):** *"Elevating Modern Living"* — Hero title treatment, geometric architectural vector frame, brand taglines, and call-to-action button.

### **Page 2: Inside Spread (3-Panel Panoramic View)**
1. **Left Panel:** *"Our Core Services"* — Comprehensive breakdown of Residential Sales, Luxury Leasing, Commercial Advisory, and Property Management.
2. **Middle Panel:** *"Featured Listings"* — Three property highlight cards (Penthouse, Coastal Villa, Hillside Estate) with pricing, key specs, and virtual tour tags.
3. **Right Panel:** *"The Apex Process & Trust"* — A 3-step client journey roadmap (Discovery → Selection → Closing) paired with an authentic client testimonial quote card.
